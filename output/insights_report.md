# Level 3 — Insights & Recommendations

## Insights
1. Weekly volume is remarkably even: 1,503-1,649 services per day (Monday lowest, Friday highest).
2. Day-of-week position explains little of the variance (r=+0.381, computed on n=7 weekly aggregates — indicative, not definitive) and the weekend effect is negligible (r=+0.127) — demand is distributed across the week.
3. Weekend days still carry 28.8% of all services (1,598/day vs 1,584 on weekdays) — weekend operations are business-critical.
4. Strong hub concentration: the top-10 source stations carry 23.1% of services; the busiest single route is CHENNAI BEACH -> TAMBARAM.

## Recommendations
1. Peak capacity: keep reserve rakes/crew for Friday (busiest day) and schedule maintenance windows on Monday (quietest day).
2. Protect weekend frequency — cutting weekend services would remove ~29% of weekly volume.
3. Prioritize infrastructure, staffing and punctuality monitoring at the dominant hubs: CST-MUMBAI, SEALDAH, CHENNAI BEACH.
4. Optimize at route level, not calendar level: since day-of-week explains little variance, focus on the top corridors and hub operations.
